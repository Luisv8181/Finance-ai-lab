A tangible fintech experiment

See your money as a living system.

Money Pulse turns a monthly budget into an interactive system map: what keeps life running, what makes life enjoyable, what builds the future, and what remains as breathing room. Change one assumption and watch the whole system respond.

Browser-only storage No bank connection No account credentials Built to be redesigned
Design thesis
“A budget should not only tell you what you spent. It should help you understand the structure, resilience, and trade-offs of your financial life.”
Prototype wellness metrics are educational heuristics, not financial advice. The lab is intentionally transparent so the group can debate the assumptions and improve them.
