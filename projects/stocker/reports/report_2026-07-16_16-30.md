### 5. Educational Insight (A quick concept or link)

**What is an ETF (Exchange Traded Fund)?**

An Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) is an investment fund that holds assets such as stocks, commodities, or bonds, and trades on stock exchanges just like regular stocks. ETFs are popular for several reasons: they offer diversification across multiple assets or sectors with a single investment, typically have lower expense ratios compared to actively managed mutual funds, and can be bought and sold throughout the trading day at market prices. The NANC ticker, often associated with an ETF tracking congressional trades, exemplifies how ETFs can be designed around specific investment themes or strategies.
