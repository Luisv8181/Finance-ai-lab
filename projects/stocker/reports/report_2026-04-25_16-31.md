### 5. Educational Insight

**What is an ETF (Exchange Traded Fund)?**

An **Exchange Traded Fund (ETF)** is a type of investment fund and exchange-traded product, meaning they trade on stock exchanges just like regular stocks. An ETF holds assets such as stocks, commodities, or bonds and generally operates with an arbitrage mechanism designed to keep its trading price close to its net asset value, although deviations can occur. ETFs allow investors to gain diversified exposure to various assets, sectors, or market segments (like the S&P 500 or even politician-tracked portfolios such as NANC) through a single, easily tradable security. They often offer lower expense ratios compared to actively managed mutual funds and provide intraday liquidity.
