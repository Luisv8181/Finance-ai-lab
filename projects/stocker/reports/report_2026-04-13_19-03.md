### 5. Educational Insight

**What is "Volume"?**

In stock trading, **Volume** refers to the total number of shares or contracts traded for a particular security or market during a given period. High trading volume often indicates significant investor interest and liquidity, meaning it's easier to buy and sell without impacting the price too much. A sudden surge in volume accompanying a price movement can signal a strong conviction behind that move, while low volume might suggest less interest or conviction. It's a crucial indicator for technical analysis.
