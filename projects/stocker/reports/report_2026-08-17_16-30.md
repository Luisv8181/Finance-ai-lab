### 5. Educational Insight

**What is Market Volatility?**

Market volatility refers to the degree of variation of a trading price series over time. High volatility means that the price of a security or market index fluctuates significantly over a short period. Low volatility means the price changes slowly and predictably. Volatility is often measured by the standard deviation or variance of returns. While it can present opportunities for traders, it also implies higher risk for investors, as unpredictable price swings can lead to rapid gains or losses. It's a key concept for understanding market risk and potential returns.
