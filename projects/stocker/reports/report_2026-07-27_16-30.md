### 5. Educational Insight

**What is an ETF? (Exchange Traded Fund)**

An Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) is a type of investment fund that holds assets such as stocks, commodities, or bonds, and trades on stock exchanges like regular stocks. ETFs are popular because they offer diversification (as they hold a basket of assets), are typically liquid, and often have lower expense ratios compared to traditional mutual funds. For example, the NANC ticker mentioned in politician trades is often an ETF designed to track the investments of a prominent political figure.
