### 5. Educational Insight

**What is Volume?**
In finance, "volume" refers to the number of shares or contracts traded in a security or an entire market during a given period. High trading volume indicates strong interest and liquidity in an asset, meaning many buyers and sellers are active. Conversely, low volume suggests less interest. Traders often use volume to confirm trends; for instance, a significant price increase on high volume is often considered a stronger signal than one on low volume.
