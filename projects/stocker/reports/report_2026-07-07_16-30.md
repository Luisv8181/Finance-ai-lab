### 5. Educational Insight

**What is "Earnings Season"?**

"Earnings season" refers to the period, usually a few weeks after the end of each fiscal quarter, when most public companies release their quarterly financial results. During this time, companies report their revenues, profits, and provide guidance for future performance. Investors and analysts closely watch these reports as they offer crucial insights into a company's financial health, growth prospects, and the overall economic landscape. Strong earnings can drive stock prices up, while disappointing results can lead to declines. This period often sees increased market volatility as investors react to the flood of new information.
