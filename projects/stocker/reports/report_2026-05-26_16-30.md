### 5. Educational Insight

**What is an ETF (Exchange Traded Fund)?**
An ETF is a type of investment fund that holds assets such as stocks, commodities, or bonds, and trades close to its net asset value over the course of the trading day on national stock exchanges. Unlike mutual funds, which are priced once at the end of the day, ETFs can be bought and sold throughout the day like regular stocks. They offer diversification and can track various indices, sectors, or even specific investment themes.
