### 5. Educational Insight

**What is "Volume" in Stock Trading?**

**Volume** refers to the number of shares or contracts traded in a security or an entire market during a given period. High trading volume indicates strong interest in a stock, which can validate price movements. For example, if a stock's price rises on high volume, it suggests that many buyers are pushing the price up. Conversely, if a stock drops on high volume, it implies many sellers are driving the price down. Low volume, on the other hand, can suggest a lack of interest, and price movements on low volume are generally considered less significant.
