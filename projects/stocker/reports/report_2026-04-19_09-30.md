### 5. Educational Insight

**What is Trading Volume?**

Trading volume refers to the total number of shares or contracts of a security that were traded during a given period. It's a key indicator of market activity and liquidity. High volume indicates strong interest in a stock, either from buyers or sellers, and generally confirms the validity of a price movement. For instance, a significant price increase on high volume is typically seen as a stronger signal than the same price increase on low volume. Conversely, a price movement on low volume might not be sustainable.
