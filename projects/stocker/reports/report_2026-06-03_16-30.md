### 5. Educational Insight

**What is Trading Volume?**
Trading volume refers to the total number of shares or contracts of a security traded during a specific period. High volume often indicates strong interest and liquidity, making it easier to buy or sell without significantly impacting the price. A large price movement on high volume is generally considered more significant than the same price movement on low volume, as it suggests more conviction behind the move. For example, NVDA's significant decline today on exceptionally high volume suggests a strong consensus among traders regarding its downward movement.
