### 5. Educational Insight

**What are ETFs (Exchange Traded Funds)?**

An **Exchange Traded Fund (ETF)** is a type of investment fund that holds a collection of assets such as stocks, bonds, or commodities, and is traded on stock exchanges, much like individual stocks. ETFs offer diversification because they track an index, sector, commodity, or other assets, but allow investors to buy and sell shares throughout the day at market-determined prices. They often have lower expense ratios compared to actively managed mutual funds and provide a convenient way to gain exposure to various market segments. The ticker NANC, mentioned in politician trades, is likely an ETF.
