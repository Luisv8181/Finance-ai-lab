### 5. Educational Insight

**What is "Volume" in stock trading?**

Volume refers to the total number of shares or contracts of a security that were traded during a specific period. High trading volume often indicates significant interest in a stock, whether from buyers or sellers. For example, NVIDIA's (NVDA) exceptionally high volume today (145.77 million shares) suggests a strong level of engagement and activity, making its price movements more significant as they are backed by broad participation. Conversely, low volume might indicate a lack of interest or conviction in a stock's price movements.
