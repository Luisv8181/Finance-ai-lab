### 5. Educational Insight

**What is a Market Correction?**
A market correction is typically defined as a 10% or greater decline in the price of a security, index, or commodity from its most recent peak. Corrections are a normal part of market cycles and can last from days to months. They are often triggered by economic concerns, geopolitical events, or shifts in investor sentiment, and are distinct from a bear market, which signifies a decline of 20% or more.
