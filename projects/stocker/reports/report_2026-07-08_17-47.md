### 5. Educational Insight

**What is Trading Volume?**

Trading volume refers to the total number of shares or contracts of a security that are traded during a particular period. High trading volume often indicates strong interest in a stock, whether from buyers or sellers. For example, NVIDIA's significant price increase today coupled with high volume suggests strong buying pressure and conviction behind the move. Conversely, a large price change on low volume might be considered less significant as it suggests fewer participants are involved. Volume can be a powerful indicator when analyzing price movements.
