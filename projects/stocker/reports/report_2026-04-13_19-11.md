### 5. Educational Insight

**What is an ETF (Exchange Traded Fund)?**

An ETF, or Exchange Traded Fund, is a type of investment fund that holds assets such as stocks, bonds, or commodities, and trades on stock exchanges much like regular stocks. ETFs offer diversification, liquidity, and generally lower fees than traditional mutual funds. The underlying assets held by an ETF determine its performance, allowing investors to gain exposure to specific market segments (like technology, emerging markets, or even politician stock picks, as seen with NANC) without buying individual securities.
