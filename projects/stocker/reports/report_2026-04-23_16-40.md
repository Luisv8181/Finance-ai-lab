### 5. Educational Insight

**What is an Exchange Traded Fund (ETF)?**

An Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) is a type of investment fund and exchange-traded product, meaning they trade on stock exchanges like regular stocks. An ETF holds assets such as stocks, commodities, or bonds, and typically operates with an arbitrage mechanism designed to keep its trading price close to its net asset value, although deviations can occur. ETFs offer diversification, often tracking a specific index (like SPY tracking the S&P 500 or QQQ tracking the Nasdaq 100), sector, commodity, or other assets, while providing the liquidity of individual stocks.
