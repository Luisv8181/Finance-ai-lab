### 5. Educational Insight

**What is an ETF?**

An Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) is a type of investment fund that holds assets such as stocks, commodities, or bonds, and trades like a common stock on a stock exchange. Unlike mutual funds, which are priced once at the end of the day, ETFs can be bought and sold throughout the trading day at market prices. They typically offer diversification, lower expense ratios compared to actively managed mutual funds, and often track a specific index, sector, commodity, or investment strategy.
