### 5. Educational Insight

**Understanding Market Corrections:**
A market correction is a sustained decline of 10% or more from a recent peak in the price of a market index or individual asset. While the current declines in tech stocks are not yet at correction levels for the broader market, significant drops in leading stocks can often precede or contribute to broader corrections. Corrections are a normal part of the market cycle, offering opportunities for long-term investors to buy quality assets at lower prices. It's crucial to differentiate between a healthy correction and a bear market (a decline of 20% or more).
