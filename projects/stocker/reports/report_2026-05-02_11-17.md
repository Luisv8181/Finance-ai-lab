### 5. Educational Insight

**What is Retail Investor Sentiment?**
Retail investor sentiment refers to the general attitude or feeling of individual, non-professional investors toward the stock market or specific stocks. This sentiment can be influenced by news, social media, economic data, and personal experiences. High retail investor sentiment often correlates with increased buying activity, particularly in popular "meme stocks," while low sentiment can lead to selling. Monitoring retail investor sentiment, especially through platforms like Reddit, can provide insights into potential short-term market movements and areas of strong speculative interest.
