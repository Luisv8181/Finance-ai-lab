### 5. Educational Insight

**What is an ETF (Exchange Traded Fund)?**

An ETF, or Exchange Traded Fund, is a type of investment fund that holds assets such as stocks, bonds, commodities, or a mix of these. It trades like a regular stock on an exchange, meaning its price can fluctuate throughout the day as it is bought and sold. ETFs are popular because they offer diversification (by holding many assets within one fund), liquidity, and often lower expense ratios compared to traditional mutual funds. The NANC ticker, for example, is an ETF designed to track a specific theme (politician trades) rather than just a broad market index.
