## 5. Educational Insight

**Understanding Trading Volume:**
Trading volume refers to the number of shares or contracts traded in a security or an entire market during a given period. High volume often indicates strong interest in a stock, whether from buyers or sellers. For instance, if a stock drops significantly on high volume (like NVDA today), it suggests a strong conviction among sellers and could signal a continuation of the downward trend. Conversely, a price increase on high volume indicates strong buying pressure. Analyzing volume alongside price action provides deeper insight into the strength or weakness of a move.
