### 5. Educational Insight

**What is "Trading Volume"?**

Trading volume refers to the total number of shares or contracts of a security that were exchanged between buyers and sellers during a given period (e.g., a day). It's a key indicator of market activity and liquidity. High volume often suggests strong interest and conviction behind a stock's price movement, whether up or down, making the move more significant. Conversely, low volume might indicate less interest or that a price change is not strongly supported, potentially making it less reliable as a trend indicator.
