### 5. Educational Insight

**What is an ETF?**

An **Exchange Traded Fund (ETF)** is a type of investment fund and exchange-traded product, i.e., they are traded on stock exchanges. ETFs hold assets such as stocks, commodities, or bonds, and generally operate with an arbitrage mechanism designed to keep their trading price close to their net asset value. ETFs offer diversification, often at a lower cost than traditional mutual funds, and can be bought and sold throughout the day like individual stocks. The NANC ETF is a specific example, designed to track a basket of stocks influenced by political trading activity.
