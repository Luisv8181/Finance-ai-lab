### 5. Educational Insight

**What is a "Market Correction"?**

A market correction is a short-term decline of 10% or more in the price of a security, an index, or an entire market from its most recent peak. Corrections are a normal part of the stock market cycle, and while they can be unsettling, they often present opportunities for long-term investors. They differ from "bear markets," which are more severe declines of 20% or more. Corrections can be caused by various factors, including economic slowdowns, rising interest rates, geopolitical events, or shifts in investor sentiment.
