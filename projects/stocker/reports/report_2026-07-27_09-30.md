### 5. Educational Insight

**What is an Initial Public Offering (IPO)?**

An Initial Public Offering (IPO) is the process by which a private corporation can offer shares to the public for the first time. It allows a company to raise capital from public investors. Going public can be a major milestone for a company, providing increased liquidity for its founders and early investors, and giving it greater access to capital markets for future growth. However, IPOs can be volatile, and it's essential for investors to thoroughly research the company's fundamentals, market conditions, and the offering's terms before investing.
