### 5. Educational Insight

**What are ETFs (Exchange Traded Funds)?**

ETFs, or Exchange Traded Funds, are a type of investment fund that holds assets such as stocks, commodities, or bonds, and trades on stock exchanges like regular stocks. ETFs are popular because they offer diversification by typically holding a basket of assets (e.g., all the stocks in the S&P 500 for SPY, or tech-heavy stocks for QQQ) and are generally liquid. They can be bought and sold throughout the trading day at market prices, unlike mutual funds which are priced once daily after the market closes. ETFs are a cost-effective way for investors to gain exposure to specific market segments, industries, or asset classes.
