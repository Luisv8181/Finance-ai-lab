### 5. Educational Insight

**What is "Earnings Season"?**
Earnings season refers to the period, typically a few weeks after the end of each fiscal quarter, when most public companies release their quarterly earnings reports. These reports provide insights into a company's financial health, including revenue, profit, and future outlook. Earnings reports are critical market movers as they can significantly impact stock prices, investor sentiment, and overall market direction. Investors closely monitor earnings season for surprises, guidance changes, and to assess the performance of sectors and the broader economy.
