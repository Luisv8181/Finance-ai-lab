**5. Educational Insight (A quick concept or link)**

**What is an ETF?**
An Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) is a type of investment fund that holds assets such as stocks, commodities, or bonds. Unlike traditional mutual funds, ETFs trade like regular stocks on stock exchanges throughout the day, meaning their price can fluctuate constantly. They offer investors diversification, often at a lower cost than mutual funds, and can be used to gain exposure to broad markets, specific sectors, or even niche themes. NANC, as mentioned in today's report, is an example of an ETF.
