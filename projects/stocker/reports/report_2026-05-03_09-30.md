### 5. Educational Insight

**Understanding Volume:**
Volume in stock trading refers to the total number of shares or contracts of a security that are traded during a given period. High trading volume often indicates strong interest and liquidity in a stock, while low volume can suggest less interest or uncertainty. Significant price movements on high volume are generally considered more reliable than those on low volume. For instance, TSLA's gain on 65.21M shares traded suggests strong conviction behind its movement today.
