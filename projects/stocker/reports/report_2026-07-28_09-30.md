## 5. Educational Insight

**What is Trading Volume?**
Trading volume quantifies the total number of shares or contracts of a specific asset that have been traded during a given period. It's a crucial indicator of market interest and liquidity. High volume typically signifies strong conviction behind a price movement, making such movements generally more reliable. Conversely, low volume might suggest weak interest, which could make a stock more susceptible to significant price swings on relatively small orders. Analyzing volume alongside price action can provide deeper insights into market sentiment and potential future direction.
