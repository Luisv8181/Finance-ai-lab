### 5. Educational Insight

**What is Volume?**

In stock trading, "volume" refers to the number of shares or contracts of a security that have been traded during a particular period (e.g., a day). High trading volume indicates strong interest in a stock, whether from buyers or sellers. For instance, a significant price change accompanied by high volume is generally considered more significant than the same price change on low volume, as it suggests broader market conviction behind the move.
