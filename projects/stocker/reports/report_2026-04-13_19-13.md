### 5. Educational Insight

**What is an ETF (Exchange Traded Fund)?**
An ETF is a type of investment fund that holds a collection of underlying assets such as stocks, bonds, or commodities, and is traded on stock exchanges just like individual stocks. ETFs offer diversification, often at a lower cost than mutual funds, and provide liquidity as they can be bought and sold throughout the trading day. They are popular for gaining exposure to specific market segments, industries, or even themes like "politician trades" without having to purchase individual securities.
