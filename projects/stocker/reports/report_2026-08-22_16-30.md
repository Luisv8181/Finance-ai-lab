### 5. Educational Insight

**Understanding Politician Stock Disclosures:**

Politicians are often required to disclose their stock trades to the public, offering a glimpse into their investment activities. While these disclosures are legal and transparent, they are closely watched by some investors who believe that politicians might have privileged information or insights into future policies that could impact certain industries or companies. These trades are not considered "insider trading" in the traditional sense, as the information is often public or pertains to broad policy initiatives rather than non-public company-specific data. Nevertheless, tracking these disclosures (sometimes via ETFs like NANC) has become a strategy for some investors seeking an "alternative insight" edge.
