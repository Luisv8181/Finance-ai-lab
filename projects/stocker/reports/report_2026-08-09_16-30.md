### 5. Educational Insight

**What is an ETF?**

An **Exchange Traded Fund (ETF)** is a type of investment fund that holds assets such as stocks, bonds, commodities, or a mix of these. ETFs are traded on stock exchanges, much like individual stocks, and their prices fluctuate throughout the day as they are bought and sold. They offer diversification benefits by allowing investors to buy a basket of assets in a single transaction, often with lower expense ratios compared to actively managed mutual funds. NANC, the ticker purchased by Nancy Pelosi, is an example of a thematic ETF.
