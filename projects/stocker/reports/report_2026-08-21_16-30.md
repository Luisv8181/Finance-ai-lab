### 5. Educational Insight

**What is Volume in Stock Trading?**
Volume refers to the number of shares or contracts traded in a security or an entire market during a given period. High trading volume indicates strong interest in a stock, whether buying or selling, and often accompanies significant price movements. Conversely, low volume suggests less interest and typically results in less volatile price changes. Understanding volume can help gauge the conviction behind a price move and the liquidity of an asset.
