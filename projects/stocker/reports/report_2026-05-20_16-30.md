### 5. Educational Insight

**What is "Trading Volume"?**

Trading volume refers to the total number of shares or contracts of a security traded during a specific period. It is a critical indicator that reflects the activity and liquidity of a stock or market. High trading volume often accompanies significant price movements, signaling strong interest and conviction among traders, while low volume may suggest a lack of interest or indecision. Analyzing volume alongside price action can provide valuable insights into the strength and sustainability of a trend.
