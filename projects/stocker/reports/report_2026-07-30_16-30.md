### 5. Educational Insight (A quick concept or link)

**What is Trading Volume?**

Trading volume refers to the total number of shares or contracts of a security that were traded during a specific period (e.g., a day). It's a crucial indicator of liquidity and market participation. High trading volume often accompanies significant price movements, suggesting stronger conviction behind the move, whether it's an increase or decrease. Conversely, low volume might indicate less interest and make price movements less reliable. Monitoring volume can help assess the strength and sustainability of a stock's price trend.
