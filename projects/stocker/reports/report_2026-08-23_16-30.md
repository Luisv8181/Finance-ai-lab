### 5. Educational Insight (A quick concept or link)

**Concept: Bond Yields and Stock Prices**

Bond yields and stock prices, especially for growth stocks, often have an inverse relationship. When bond yields rise (meaning bond prices fall), bonds become more attractive relative to stocks, as they offer a higher, less volatile return. This can lead investors to sell stocks and buy bonds, putting downward pressure on stock prices. For growth stocks, higher yields also mean that the present value of their future earnings (which are often far out in the future) decreases, making them less appealing. Conversely, falling bond yields can make stocks more attractive.
