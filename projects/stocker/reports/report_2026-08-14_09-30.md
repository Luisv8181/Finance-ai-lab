### 5. Educational Insight

**What is an ETF?**
An **Exchange-Traded Fund (ETF)** is a type of investment fund that is traded on stock exchanges, much like individual stocks. ETFs typically hold a basket of assets such as stocks, bonds, or commodities, and their price fluctuates throughout the trading day as they are bought and sold. They offer investors diversification, often at a lower cost than traditional mutual funds, and provide liquidity as they can be easily traded. Examples from today's report include SPY (S&P 500 ETF), QQQ (Nasdaq 100 ETF), and the recently traded NANC.
