## 5. Educational Insight

**What is an ETF (Exchange Traded Fund)?**

An ETF is a type of investment fund traded on stock exchanges, much like regular stocks. An ETF holds assets such as stocks, commodities, or bonds, and typically tracks an underlying index, sector, commodity, or other assets. ETFs can offer diversification, lower costs, and tax efficiency compared to mutual funds, and their prices fluctuate throughout the trading day as they are bought and sold.
