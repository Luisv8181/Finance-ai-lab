### 5. Educational Insight

**What is "Volume" in stock trading?**

Volume refers to the number of shares or contracts traded in a security or an entire market during a given period. High trading volume indicates strong interest in a stock, whether buying or selling, and can validate price movements. For example, a significant price increase on high volume suggests a strong upward trend, while a price increase on low volume might indicate a weaker, less sustainable move. Conversely, a sharp price drop on high volume points to strong selling pressure. Monitoring volume alongside price action provides valuable context for understanding market dynamics.
