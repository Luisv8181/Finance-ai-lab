### 5. Educational Insight

**What is an Exchange Traded Fund (ETF)?**

An Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) is a type of investment fund that holds assets such as stocks, bonds, or commodities, and trades like a common stock on a stock exchange. ETFs offer diversification, often tracking an index, sector, commodity, or other asset, but with the flexibility of being bought and sold throughout the trading day at market prices, unlike mutual funds which are priced once daily. NANC, for example, is an ETF designed to track the investments made by members of the U.S. Congress.
