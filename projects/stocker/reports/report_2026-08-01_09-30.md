### 5. Educational Insight

**What is Trading Volume?**

Trading volume refers to the total number of shares or contracts of a security that were traded during a given period. High trading volume often indicates significant investor interest and liquidity for a particular stock. For instance, NVDA and AAPL showed high volumes today (over 130M shares each), suggesting active trading and strong interest in these companies. Low volume, conversely, can suggest a lack of interest or liquidity.
