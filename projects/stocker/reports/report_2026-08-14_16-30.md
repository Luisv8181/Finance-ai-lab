### 5. Educational Insight

**What is Trading Volume?**

Trading volume refers to the total number of shares or contracts of a security that were traded during a given period. It's a key indicator of market activity and liquidity. High volume often accompanies significant price movements, suggesting strong interest (either buying or selling) from investors. Conversely, low volume during a price move might indicate less conviction behind that move. Analyzing volume alongside price action can help confirm trends and signal potential reversals.
