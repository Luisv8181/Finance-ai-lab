### 5. Educational Insight

**What is Volume?**
In finance, **Volume** refers to the total number of shares or contracts of a security that are traded over a given period, typically a day. High trading volume often indicates strong interest in a stock, whether buying or selling, and can validate price movements. For example, a significant price drop on very high volume (as seen with NVDA today) suggests a strong conviction among sellers. Conversely, a price increase on high volume indicates strong buying interest. Low volume, on the other hand, can suggest a lack of interest or conviction in a security's price movement.
