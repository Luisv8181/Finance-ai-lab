### 5. Educational Insight

**What is an Exchange-Traded Fund (ETF)?**

An Exchange-Traded Fund (ETF) is a type of investment fund that holds assets such as stocks, bonds, or commodities. It trades on stock exchanges, much like a regular stock. ETFs offer diversification by holding a basket of securities and typically have lower expense ratios than mutual funds. Examples in our report include SPY (tracks the S&P 500), QQQ (tracks the Nasdaq 100), and NANC (tracks congressional trading activity). Investing in an ETF allows you to gain exposure to a broad market index, a specific sector, or a unique investment theme with a single trade.
