### 5. Educational Insight

**What is "Volume" in Stock Trading?**

Volume refers to the number of shares or contracts of a security that are traded during a specific period, typically a day. High trading volume often indicates significant investor interest in a stock, and a large number of buyers and sellers actively participating. It can also suggest that a price movement (up or down) is more significant and sustainable if it's accompanied by high volume, as it represents strong conviction from the market participants. Conversely, low volume might indicate less interest and potentially less reliable price movements.
