### 5. Educational Insight

**What is Market Capitalization (Market Cap)?**

Market capitalization is the total value of a company's outstanding shares. It is calculated by multiplying the current share price by the total number of outstanding shares. Market cap is often used to classify companies as large-cap, mid-cap, or small-cap, providing a quick measure of a company's size and perceived value by the market. For example, Apple (AAPL) is a "mega-cap" company due to its extremely high market capitalization, making it a significant component of major indices.
