### 5. Educational Insight

**What is "Volume"?**

In stock market terms, **volume** refers to the number of shares or contracts traded in a security or an entire market during a given period. High trading volume often indicates significant interest in a stock, whether buying or selling. It can validate price movements – a strong price increase on high volume is generally considered more significant than the same increase on low volume. Conversely, low volume might suggest less conviction behind a price change. For example, today, NVDA's exceptionally high volume suggests considerable market attention and activity around the stock.
