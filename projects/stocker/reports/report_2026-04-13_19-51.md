### 5. Educational Insight

**What is Trading Volume?**

**Trading volume** refers to the total number of shares or contracts of a particular security that were traded during a given period, typically a day. It is a crucial indicator for analyzing market activity and assessing the strength of a price movement.

**Why is it important?**
*   **Confirmation:** High volume often confirms the validity of a price trend. A significant price increase on high volume suggests strong conviction behind the move, while a price increase on low volume might indicate a lack of broad interest and potentially a weaker trend.
*   **Liquidity:** High volume indicates high liquidity, meaning it's easier to buy or sell the security without significantly impacting its price.
*   **Reversals:** Unusually high volume at a price extreme (a peak after an uptrend or a trough after a downtrend) can sometimes signal a potential trend reversal, as it indicates strong buying or selling pressure exhausting itself.

In today's report, NVDA's substantial volume accompanying its price gain suggests robust demand and strong market interest in the stock.
