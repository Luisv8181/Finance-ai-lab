### 5. Educational Insight

**What is Geopolitical Risk in the Stock Market?**

Geopolitical risk refers to the impact of political events or instability in one country or region on the financial markets, economies, and businesses globally. This can include conflicts, trade disputes, policy changes, elections, or natural disasters that have broader international implications.

**Impact:** Geopolitical risks can introduce uncertainty, leading to increased market volatility, currency fluctuations, supply chain disruptions, and changes in investor sentiment. Companies with significant international operations or dependencies on specific regions are often more susceptible. Understanding these risks is crucial for investors to anticipate potential market shifts and adjust portfolios accordingly.
