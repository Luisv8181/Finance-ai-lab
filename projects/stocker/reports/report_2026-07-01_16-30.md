### 5. Educational Insight

**What is an Exchange Traded Fund (ETF)?**

An Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) is a type of investment fund that holds assets such as stocks, commodities, or bonds, and trades on stock exchanges like regular stocks. ETFs offer diversification, often at a lower cost than mutual funds, and can track various indices (like SPY tracking the S&P 500, or QQQ tracking the Nasdaq 100), sectors, or even specific investment strategies (like NANC tracking politician trades). They combine the diversification of a mutual fund with the tradability of a stock.
