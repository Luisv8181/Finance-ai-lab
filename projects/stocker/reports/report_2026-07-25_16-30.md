### 5. Educational Insight

**What is "Volume" in Stock Trading?**

"Volume" refers to the number of shares or contracts traded in a security or an entire market during a given period. It's a key indicator of market activity and liquidity. High volume often accompanies significant price movements, indicating strong conviction from buyers or sellers. Conversely, low volume can suggest a lack of interest or indecision in the market. Traders and investors use volume to confirm trends, identify potential reversals, and assess the strength of price action.
