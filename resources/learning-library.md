# Learning Library

The goal is not to consume endless content. Use these sources to answer a question, then bring the result back to the lab.

## Finance foundations

### Aswath Damodaran — valuation and corporate finance
https://www.youtube.com/@AswathDamodaranonValuation

Use when:
- talking about valuation;
- separating narrative from assumptions;
- learning corporate-finance mechanics.

### MIT OpenCourseWare
https://www.youtube.com/@mitocw

Use when:
- someone needs a structured foundation instead of another commentary video.

### Patrick Boyle
https://www.youtube.com/@PBoyle

Use when:
- a market story involves derivatives, hedge funds, currencies, market structure, leverage, or financial history.

Rule: treat commentary as a starting point. Check important factual claims against primary/strong sources.

## Suggested consumption rule

For anything brought to a meeting, prepare only:

1. **Claim** — what is the core idea?
2. **Evidence** — why should we believe it?
3. **Counterpoint** — what might be wrong/incomplete?
4. **Connection** — how does it relate to finance, AI, or our build?
5. **Action** — is there anything worth building/testing?

No one gets credit for merely having watched more videos.
