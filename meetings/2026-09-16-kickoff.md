# Kickoff — September 16, 2026

## Goal

Turn the biweekly conversation into a lab that compounds knowledge without losing the friendship/business-update part of the meeting.

## 90-minute agenda

### 0–12 min — Updates

Each person gets about four minutes:
- one meaningful life/career update;
- one thing they are trying to learn/change;
- one decision or question for the group.

### 12–22 min — Define what this lab should become

Discuss:
1. What makes this meeting worth protecting every two weeks?
2. Six months from now, what would we be proud to show someone?
3. What balance do we want among finance, AI, coding, business, and life?
4. How much prep is realistic?

Suggested default: one primary reading + one small build objective every two weeks.

### 22–42 min — Reading: tokenization moves toward production

Primary source:

**DTCC — “DTCC Turns Tokenization into Reality: U.S. Trades Successfully Processed Using DTC-Tokenized Assets”**  
July 15, 2026  
https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/dtcc-turns-tokenization-into-reality

Discussion questions:
- What exactly was tokenized?
- Which existing post-trade functions remain necessary?
- Which reconciliations could disappear?
- Which new controls/reconciliations appear?
- Where do custody, identity, permissions, legal ownership, and settlement finality live?
- Does a faster asset leg help if cash/funding is not equally programmable?
- Who captures the economic benefit?
- What would have to be true for tokenization to become boring infrastructure rather than a special product?

Optional companion:

**DTCC — “Tokenized Collateral Could Unlock Billions in Capital and Transform Liquidity Management”**  
May 13, 2026  
https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/tokenized-collateral-could-unlock-billions-in-capital-and-transform-liquidity-management

### 42–50 min — Broken Assumption / Blooper

Prompt:

> Name one technology or market prediction you accepted too easily in the past. What evidence would you demand now?

Possible examples:
- “Blockchain will remove all financial intermediaries.”
- “AI agents only need smarter models.”
- “Faster settlement is better in every dimension.”
- “A stock moved after a headline, therefore the headline caused the move.”

The point is to train the habit of finding hidden assumptions.

### 50–60 min — AI opportunity discussion

Discussion starter:

Reuters reported on September 15, 2026 that coding-agent company Factory raised $200 million at a $5 billion valuation.

Question:

> If generating code becomes abundant, what becomes scarce?

Possibilities to debate:
- good requirements;
- domain expertise;
- testing;
- architecture;
- data access;
- permissions;
- integration;
- judgment;
- accountability.

### 60–82 min — Build: Market Watcher v0

Open `projects/market-watcher/README.md`.

Decide:
1. default instruments;
2. what counts as a “market update”;
3. what the system should teach every run;
4. what evidence must be saved;
5. what would make an explanation bad even if it sounds intelligent.

### 82–90 min — Commitments

Assign one owner each for:
- data / deterministic analytics;
- agent/explanation layer;
- next reading + meeting note.
